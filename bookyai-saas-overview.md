# Booky AI - SaaS Product Overview

## What is Booky AI?

Booky AI is an AI-powered appointment booking and scheduling automation platform that works 24/7 to help businesses manage their appointments without manual intervention. It serves as an intelligent virtual receptionist that handles scheduling through natural conversation.

## Core Value Proposition

The platform transforms business appointment booking by letting AI handle all scheduling tasks, allowing business owners to focus on growth rather than administrative work. It operates continuously (24/7) and uses natural language processing to interact with clients.

## How It Works

1. **Client Reaches Out** - Customer calls, texts, or chats with the business number
2. **AI Engagement** - AI assistant understands needs through natural conversation
3. **Smart Scheduling** - System finds optimal time slots based on availability
4. **Confirmation & Reminders** - Appointment confirmed, synced to calendar, with automated follow-ups
