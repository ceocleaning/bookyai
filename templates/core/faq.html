Getting Started

Getting started is simple! Sign up for a free 14-day trial, connect your calendar (Google, Outlook, or iCal), customize your AI assistant's responses, and you're ready to go. The entire setup takes less than 10 minutes, and our onboarding wizard guides you through each step.

Features & Capabilities

We integrate with Google Calendar, Outlook, iCal, Zoom, Microsoft Teams, Slack, Salesforce, HubSpot, Stripe, and many more. Our REST API also allows custom integrations with your existing tools. New integrations are added regularly based on customer requests.

Billing & Pricing

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover), PayPal, and ACH bank transfers for annual plans. Enterprise customers can also pay via invoice with NET 30 terms.
Yes! You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time from your dashboard. Upgrades take effect immediately, while downgrades take effect at the start of your next billing cycle. We'll prorate any charges accordingly.
Yes! We offer a 20% discount when you choose annual billing. This applies to all plans (Starter, Professional, and Enterprise). You can switch to annual billing at any time.
We'll notify you when you reach 90% of your limit. If you exceed it, you can either upgrade to a higher plan or pay a small overage fee per additional booking. You can set your preference in your account settings.
Yes! We offer a 14-day money-back guarantee on your first subscription. If you're not satisfied within the first 14 days, we'll issue a full refund—no questions asked. See our Refund Policy for details.
